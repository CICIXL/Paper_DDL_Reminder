Privacy

{{ site_name }} stores as little as it can: your email address and the conferences you chose.

What is stored

If you subscribe: your email address, the areas and conferences you ticked, when you signed up and confirmed, and a record of which reminders were already sent to you so you do not get them twice.

If you only browse: nothing. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

What it is used for

Only to send the reminders you asked for. Your address is never sold, shared for marketing, or used for anything else.

Who handles it

The site and its database are hosted by Render. Emails are delivered by Resend. Both act only on this site's instructions. Like any web host, they keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses.

Deleting your data

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Using it deletes your address and choices immediately. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days.
