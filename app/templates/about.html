About {{ site_name }}

One place for the dates that matter when you are writing a paper, and a nudge before each of them.

What it does

{{ site_name }} lists abstract, submission, rebuttal, decision and conference dates for major machine learning, NLP, vision, systems and AI-for-science conferences. Pick the ones you follow and you get a short email before each date, and when a date moves.

Where the dates come from

Dates are entered by hand from official conference sites and synced every day with the community tracker ccfddl. A date tagged auto-updated or unconfirmed came from a tracker and has not been checked by a person.

Conferences change their dates, and trackers lag or make mistakes. Treat this site as a reminder, and confirm every date on the official site before you rely on it.

How reminders work

Paper deadlines get a reminder 30, 7 and 1 days ahead. Abstract and commitment deadlines get one 7 days and 1 day ahead. Rebuttal windows, decisions and the conference itself get one each. Everything due on the same day arrives as a single email.

There is no account. Every email carries your private link for changing what you follow or unsubscribing.
